Common questions

How do I get gift ideas?

Open the Find Gifts tab, choose an occasion, describe the person you are shopping for, and optionally set a budget. Tap Find Gift Ideas and you will get a set of suggestions tailored to what you entered.

Why are my suggestions not what I expected?

The more detail you give about the person — their interests, age, and what they already own — the closer the suggestions get. Try adding a hobby or two and running the search again.

Do I need an account?

No. Gift Box has no sign-up or login. You can use every feature straight after installing.

Where do the shopping links go?

Suggestions link to Amazon and eBay. Gift Box earns a small commission if you buy through those links, at no extra cost to you.

How do occasion reminders work?

In the Occasions tab you can save birthdays, anniversaries, and custom dates. If you allow notifications, Gift Box reminds you three days before and again on the day. Yearly dates repeat automatically.

I am not getting reminders.

Open the Settings app, find Gift Box, tap Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications. Reminders only work while notifications are enabled.

What is the tracking prompt when I first open the app?

Gift Box shows banner ads. Apple requires apps to ask before using your advertising identifier to personalise them. Choosing Ask App Not to Track is completely fine — the app works exactly the same, and you will still see ads, just less relevant ones.

How do I delete my data?

Your searches, saved occasions, and history are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it. Nothing is kept on a server after deletion.
